About

I am a full-time political science student at Purdue University Fort Wayne with professional experience in legal operations, political campaigns, and consulting. I currently serve as Operations Coordinator at Tipton Law Group, where I support day-to-day firm administration, municipal client coordination, and internal systems under the direction of the managing attorney.

In the fall of 2024, I founded Bowmax LLC, a small consulting practice providing operational and administrative support to local political campaigns and community organizations. My background includes campaign field work, client services, and organizational support across Northeast Indiana. My academic and professional interests center on law, local government, and the institutional systems that support effective public service.
